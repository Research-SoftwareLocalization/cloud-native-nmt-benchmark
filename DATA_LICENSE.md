# Data Provenance and Licensing

## Source strings
The 10,228 English source UI strings used in this study originate from the KDE
desktop environment's localization files, as collected and distributed through the
OPUS KDE4 corpus (Tiedemann, 2012; https://opus.nlpl.eu/datasets/KDE4). KDE software
and its associated translation catalogs are distributed predominantly under the GNU
GPL and LGPL licenses. Users reusing these strings should consult the KDE licensing
terms (https://community.kde.org/Policies/Licensing_Policy) and the OPUS terms of use
to confirm the exact conditions that apply to their use case.

## Translation outputs
The NMT translation outputs in this repository were generated by the authors:
- Open-source systems (NLLB-200, IndicTrans2): produced locally under the model
  licenses (NLLB-200: CC-BY-NC 4.0; IndicTrans2: MIT). These outputs are provided as
  a research artifact for reproducibility.
- Commercial systems (Google Translate baseline, Microsoft Translator): generated via the respective services. The output files are
  retained as a frozen record of what each service produced on the recorded run
  dates. Reuse of commercial-system outputs is subject to each provider's terms of
  service.
